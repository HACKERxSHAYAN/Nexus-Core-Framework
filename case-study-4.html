Project Overview

NeuralSystems wanted to revolutionize how businesses interact with their data. The goal was to create an analytics platform that could be queried using natural language, making data accessible to everyone regardless of technical expertise.

We developed an AI-powered platform that uses large language models to understand queries, generate SQL, and present results in intuitive visualizations. The platform also includes predictive analytics and anomaly detection.
